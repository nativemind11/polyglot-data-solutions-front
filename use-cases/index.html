What we build for AI teams

Representative project scopes across voice, language and evaluation. Every engagement is tailored — tell us about yours.

Automotive

In-car voice assistants

Challenge
Reliable command recognition in noisy cabins and across regional dialects.
Approach
Scripted commands and natural phrasings recorded by a balanced speaker pool, under cabin conditions such as engine, air-conditioning and speed noise.
Deliverables
Validated audio with speaker and environment metadata, ready for ASR training.
Contact centers

Conversational speech for call-center AI

Challenge
Models that handle real, overlapping, accented customer conversations.
Approach
Spontaneous two-speaker conversations, transcribed with timestamps and speaker diarization by native-speaker transcribers.
Deliverables
Aligned transcripts and audio, in your schema and style guide.
Text-to-speech

Multilingual TTS voice talent

Challenge
Natural, consistent voices in each target language and style.
Approach
Casting, briefing and studio-grade recording of professional voice talent with consistent delivery across long scripts.
Deliverables
Clean, trimmed, named recordings with full scripts and consent documentation.
Wake words

Wake-word & command datasets

Challenge
Detecting a wake word across voices, distances and devices — without false triggers.
Approach
Hundreds of speakers saying wake words and near-miss phrases, at varied distances and on varied devices.
Deliverables
Balanced, labeled positives and negatives with metadata for threshold tuning.
LLM evaluation

Multilingual LLM evaluation & RLHF

Challenge
Finding quality and safety gaps in languages your team does not read.
Approach
Calibrated native-speaker raters rank, rate and red-team model outputs against clear rubrics.
Deliverables
Per-language results, error categories and examples you can act on.
Dialect data

Dialect-rich Arabic speech corpora

Challenge
Arabic models that understand how people actually speak, not only the standard form.
Approach
Targeted recruitment across Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine and Maghrebi speakers with quotas by age group and gender.
Deliverables
Balanced dialect datasets with demographic metadata and documented consent.
