FAQ

Data Annotation — common questions

Can you work inside our annotation tool?

Yes. We can work in your platform, a commercial tool or a custom workflow — whichever keeps your pipeline simple.

How do you keep labels consistent across a large team?

Written guidelines, a calibration pilot, gold-standard test items and ongoing reviewer sampling keep annotators aligned, with feedback to individuals when they drift.

Do you annotate sensitive content?

Only where agreed in advance, with appropriate safeguards, content warnings and opt-outs for annotators.
