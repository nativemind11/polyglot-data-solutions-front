Translation & Localization: common questions

Do you translate AI training data and prompts?

Yes — preserving intent, difficulty and label integrity is a specialty, not an afterthought.

Can you adapt content between Arabic dialects?

Yes. We adapt between Modern Standard Arabic and regional dialects, or between dialects, using native speakers of each.

How do you keep terminology consistent?

Shared glossaries and style guides are built with you at the start and enforced in review.
